Timothy Leary (Complete system)

**Epistemology:**
==the branch of philosophy focused on the nature, origin, scope, and limits of human knowledge==. Known as the "theory of knowledge," it investigates what it means to know something, how knowledge is acquired (via perception, reason, or testimony), and how to justify beliefs. It distinguishes true knowledge from mere opinion or belief

**Ontology**
1. the branch of metaphysics dealing with the nature of being.
2. a set of concepts and categories in a subject area or domain that shows their properties and the relations between them.
	"what's new about our ontology is that it is created automatically from large datasets"

**Teleology**
    Philosophy
    the explanation of phenomena in terms of the purpose they serve rather than of the cause by which they arise.
    "no theory of history can do without teleology"
        Theology
        the doctrine of design and purpose in the material world.

Oprindelse
mid 18th century (denoting the branch of philosophy that deals with ends or final causes): from modern Latin teleologia, from Greek telos ‘end’ + -logia (see -logy).

**Eschatology:**
==the branch of theology concerned with "last things," including death, judgment, the afterlife, and the final destiny of humankind and the world==. Rooted in the Greek _eschatos_ ("last"), it covers individual afterlife (personal eschatology) and cosmic events like the second coming of Jesus, the resurrection, and the new creation
